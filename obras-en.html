Public works on hold

An analysis of public works projects in Peru that remained stalled despite being close to completion.

100% complete on paper, still stalled

As of June 30, 2026, 23 projects reported 100% construction progress and remained on the Comptroller General’s list of stalled public works.

Of the 2,262 projects in the inventory, 281 reported more than 90% construction progress. Budget records could be matched to 267 of those projects. 155 had no recorded allocation for 2026.
